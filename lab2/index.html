Lab 2: Multivariate Visualization with D3

This visualization explores the relationship among population, temperature, development level, and region.

Design Justification

Mapping:

  • population → x-position
  • temp_c → y-position
  • development_level → circle size
  • region → color

Rationale: Population is a ratio variable, so a linear x-axis makes magnitude comparisons and trends clear. Temperature (interval) maps naturally to vertical position to reveal correlations with population. Development level is ordinal (Low, Medium, High). So circle size conveys rank order without implying exact numeric differences. Region is nominal, so categorical colors let viewers quickly group and separate cities by area.

Viewer goals: The plot should reveal regional clustering. Do cities from the same region share similar temperatures or sizes? What is the relationship between population and temperature (positive, negative, or none)? How does development level distribute across the two continuous variables (population and temperature)? Tooltips provide exact values and city names for precise inspection.
